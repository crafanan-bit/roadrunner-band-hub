A Message from the Director

Welcome to the 2026-2027 school year! My name is Colton Rafanan and I'm proud to serve as the Band Director of this program. Our goal is to guide students to success through musicianship, building self-esteem, social skills, and creativity in a safe learning environment. I look forward to a year of hard-work, learning, and fun!

Grading Categories

Grading Scale

  • A (85-100%): Exceeds Proficiency
  • B (70-84%): Meets Proficiency
  • C (55-69%): Approaching Proficiency
  • D (40-54%): Partially Proficient
  • F (0-39%): Below Proficiency

Required Materials

Please bring these items to every class & rehearsal.

  • Instrument & all accessories (reeds, oil, etc.)
  • Music Folder with all music
  • Pencil (not a pen!)
  • Journal (Class time only)
  • Towel/Rag (for brass players)

Band Fundraising

Where Fundraising Dollars Go

Funds raised throughout the 2026–2027 school year directly benefit our music students by covering festival entry fees, instrument repairs, new sheet music purchases, transportation costs, and marching band supplies.

Upcoming: MoneyDolly Campaign

Future Event

MoneyDolly is a app-based fundraising platform that allows family and friends to directly support our band program digitally—no order forms or cash collection required.

How It Works for Students & Families:

  • Download the MoneyDolly app from the App Store or Google Play Store.
  • Join our campaign using our official TRMS Band Code (will be distributed at launch).
  • Share your student's donation link via text, email, and social media.
Status: Campaign dates, access codes, and kick-off details are currently being scheduled and will be shared via Remind soon.

Additional Fundraisers (TBD)

We are actively exploring additional fundraising opportunities (such as restaurant profit nights and event concessions) for later in the school year. Specific events, dates, and instructions are To Be Determined (TBD) and will be posted here as finalized.
